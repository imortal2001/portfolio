Sari-Sari Tycoon Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026 · Developer: Louie Jay A. Ceniza · Contact: louiejayceniza15@gmail.com

The short version
  • Your game progress is saved only on your phone. I don't run a server and I don't receive your progress.
  • There's no account to create and the game doesn't ask for your name, email or location.
  • Optional ads come from Google AdMob, which collects device information to show and measure ads.
  • Purchases go through Google Play. RevenueCat checks that each purchase is real. I never see your payment details.

Information that stays on your device

Sari-Sari Tycoon stores your store, coins, upgrades, settings and game statistics on your phone. This information is not sent to me or to anyone else by the game. You can delete it at any time with Town → Erase progress, or by uninstalling the game. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include this game data in your own Google backup, under Google's control.

If you turn on reminders (they're off unless you choose them), the game schedules them on your phone. They never go through a server.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional video ads: you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game reward, and no ad plays unless you tap for it. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • your IP address and the approximate location it suggests,
  • information about your device and how you interact with the ads, and
  • crash and performance diagnostics.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later in Town → Privacy choices. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your phone's settings (on most Android phones: Settings → Google → Ads).

Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases (Google Play and RevenueCat)

You can buy optional items such as No Ads and store decorations. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play: I never receive your card or bank details. To confirm that a purchase is real and to restore it on a new phone, the game uses RevenueCat, which receives your purchase history (which items, when, the store's receipt) linked to a random app user ID. It does not receive your name or email from the game. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

Sari-Sari Tycoon is not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact me and I will help remove it.

Your choices and requests

You can erase your game data in the game or by uninstalling it, control ad personalization as described above, and ask Google or RevenueCat directly about the data they hold. For any question or request about this policy, email louiejayceniza15@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts handling data differently, I will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect.
